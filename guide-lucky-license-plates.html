Lucky license plates

Quick answer: Single digits, all-8 plates and short lucky combos (8, 88, 28, 168) are the most prized. Hong Kong's Transport Department runs regular auctions where plates have fetched HK$10M+. Proceeds go to charity.

Why plates are so valuable

A plate is public, permanent and tied to status. In Hong Kong, a plate reading "28" (易发, "easy prosperity" in Cantonese) was widely reported to fetch about HK$18 million at auction in 2016, and single-character plates have gone even higher. Mainland cities and Macau auction special numbers too.

How to pick yours

  • Letters don't matter, only the digits.
  • Short beats long. Fewer digits read like a statement.
  • Birth years, anniversaries or zodiac digits give a personal touch.
  • Avoid 4, 14, 74 and 250.
