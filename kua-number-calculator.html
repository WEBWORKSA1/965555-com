Kua Number Calculator

Your Kua (Gua) number comes from the Eight Mansions school of feng shui. It gives your four best directions for desk, bed and front door.

How the Kua number is calculated

Use the solar year: if you were born before February 4, use the previous year. Add the last two digits of the year and reduce them to one digit.

  • Male: 10 − n (born before 2000) or 9 − n (born 2000+). A result of 5 becomes 2.
  • Female: n + 5 (before 2000) or n + 6 (2000+), reduced to one digit. A result of 5 becomes 8.

East group: 1, 3, 4, 9. West group: 2, 6, 7, 8. People in the same group tend to share favourable directions.
