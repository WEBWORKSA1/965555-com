Privacy Policy

Effective: September 29, 2026

1. Who we are

965555.com ("we") is an independent informational website. Contact us via our contact form.

2. What we collect

  • Information you submit in forms: name, email, numbers, birth date if provided, messages. It is used only for the purpose stated on the form.

  • Usage data via cookies and similar technologies (analytics, advertising), if you consent.

3. Form processing

Form submissions are relayed by a third-party form service (FormSubmit) to our private inbox. They are not published.

4. Advertising (Google AdSense)

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's advertising cookies enable it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You may opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

5. Analytics

We may use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate usage, only after you accept cookies.

6. Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. YouTube's privacy policy then applies.

7. Donations

Payments are processed by PayPal or other listed platforms. We never see or store card details.

8. Your rights

Under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA and similar laws you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your data, and opt out of the "sale/sharing" of personal information. Contact us via the form. We do not sell personal information.

9. Children

The site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the effective date above when we do.
