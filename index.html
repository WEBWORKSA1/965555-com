Frequently asked questions

What is the luckiest number in Chinese culture?

8. Its pronunciation (bā) sounds like 发 (fā), "to prosper". That's why the Beijing Olympics opened on 08-08-2008 at 8:08 pm, and why plates and phone numbers full of 8s sell for fortunes.

Why is 4 unlucky in China?

四 (sì) sounds almost like 死 (sǐ), "death". Many buildings in China, Hong Kong, Taiwan and Singapore skip the 4th, 14th and 24th floors, and 4 is avoided in gifts and phone numbers.

What does 965555 mean?

9-6 reads as "long-lasting and smooth" (久, 溜). 5555 is the internet code for sobbing (呜呜呜呜). One folk reading of 965 is 就留我, "just keep me". We read it as "from tears to lasting fortune". See our full guide.

How is the lucky score calculated?

We average traditional digit values (8 highest, 4 lowest), add or subtract famous combos (168, 518, 520, 14, 250), and add pattern bonuses such as repeated endings. The method is on the checker page. It is for entertainment and cultural education.

Is this based on Mandarin or Cantonese?

Both. Most meanings come from Mandarin, but we flag Cantonese readings where they differ. For example, 3 sounds like 生 (life) and 5 like 唔 (not) in Cantonese.
