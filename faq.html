Frequently asked questions

Numbers & culture

What is the luckiest Chinese number?

8, because 八 (bā) sounds like 发 (fā), "prosper".

Why do buildings skip the 4th floor?

四 (sì) sounds like 死 (sǐ), "death". Many buildings skip 4, 14, 24 and sometimes 13.

What does 520 mean?

我爱你, "I love you". May 20 is celebrated as an internet Valentine's Day in China.

What does 555 mean in Chinese texting?

Crying: 呜呜呜 (wū wū wū). More 5s mean more tears, often playfully.
