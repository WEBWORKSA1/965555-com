# 965555.com — Research Brief

## 1. What "965555" means in Chinese number culture

| Digit | Mandarin | Common homophone / association | Tone in number culture |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | jiǔ 九 | 久 jiǔ, "long-lasting, eternal"; also the imperial number | Lucky |
| 6 | liù 六 | 溜 liù, "smooth, slick"; 禄 lù, "fortune/official salary"; 666 = "awesome" in gamer slang | Lucky |
| 5 | wǔ 五 | 呜 wū, crying (555 / 5555 = "boo-hoo" in internet slang); 我 wǒ, "me"; 五福 five blessings; 五行 five elements; Cantonese *ng5* ≈ 唔, "not" | Mixed |

**Readings of 965555 (all informal and folk readings, none canonical):**
- **9-6 = "long-lasting and smooth"** (久 + 溜/顺). A positive opening pair.
- **965 ≈ 就留我 (jiù liú wǒ), "just keep me"**. This is a playful homophone chain of the kind common in number slang (compare 520 = 我爱你).
- **5555 = 呜呜呜呜**, the textbook internet code for sobbing, and the most recognisable part of the number. Brand angle: "from tears (5555) to lasting fortune (9-6)".
- **Pattern value.** Six digits, with **no 4 and no 0**. That puts it in the "premium" subset of 262,144 6N .coms that Chinese investors prefer (NamePros). It also ends in the quad pattern **AAAA (5555)**, and repeating patterns sell above random 6N names.

**Economy and people:**
- Chinese internet users value numeric domains because pinyin is ambiguous and typing is slow. Numbers need no translation. Examples: 163.com, 360.com, 58.com, 4399.com (MediaOptions).
- Numeric domain sales: 55.com $2.3M, 114.com $2.1M, 88888.com $245K. In 2015, 6N .coms moved in bulk at $160–$250+, and patterned names sell higher (NamePros, DNW).
- Number luck carries real prices. A Hong Kong plate with only the digit 8 sold for about US$6M+. The Beijing Olympics opened 08-08-08 at 8:08 pm. Buildings skip floor 4. Lucky phone numbers and plates are auctioned by the state in China, Hong Kong, Singapore and Taiwan.

**Conclusion.** 965555 is not an "8" money number, so selling it on fortune alone is weak. Its strength is a memorable pattern plus the most famous number-slang code in Chinese (555). That makes it a natural brand for a **number-meaning / number-luck platform**: the name *is* a decodable number, which is exactly what the site does.
