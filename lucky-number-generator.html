Tips for choosing a lucky number

  • End strong. Endings with 8, 6 or 9 matter most, because that's what people remember.
  • Avoid 4 and 14/74/748 if your audience is Chinese, Hong Kong, Taiwanese, Singaporean or Malaysian.
  • Patterns beat randomness. AAAA endings, ABAB repeats and rising runs are easier to recall and resell.
