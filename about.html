About 965555.com

Our name is itself a puzzle: 9-6 reads as "lasting and smooth", and 5555 is Chinese internet slang for sobbing. We turn number confusion ("is my number unlucky?") into clarity and a little fortune.

Our mission

To be the clearest, most useful and most honest resource on how numbers are perceived across the Chinese-speaking world, for travellers, families, couples, investors, marketers and the curious.

Our method

  • Meanings are drawn from established linguistic and cultural references and flagged when they are regional or slang.
  • Our scoring method is published openly on the checker page.
  • We separate culture from claims: number luck is tradition and perception, not prediction.

How we're funded

Advertising (Google AdSense), sponsorships, affiliate partnerships, optional premium services and supporter donations. Editorial content is independent of advertisers.
