Chinese Lucky Numbers: The Complete Guide

Why a string of digits can be worth millions, or be quietly skipped in every elevator.

Quick answer: The luckiest numbers are 8 (prosperity), 6 (smooth), 9 (long-lasting) and 2 (pairs). The unluckiest is 4 (sounds like death). Popular combos include 168 (prosper all the way), 518 (I will prosper), 888 (triple wealth), 520 (I love you) and 1314 (a lifetime). Avoid 14, 74, 748 and 250.

Why numbers carry luck

Chinese is a tonal language with many homophones. A digit that sounds like a good word becomes a good-luck charm, and a digit that sounds like a bad word gets avoided. Numerology also draws on older traditions such as yin-yang (even vs odd), the Five Elements and the imperial symbolism of 9.

The lucky numbers

8: prosperity (发)

八 bā sounds like 发 fā, as in 发财 "get rich". The 2008 Beijing Olympics opened 08-08-08 at 8:08:08 pm. A Hong Kong license plate reading "28" was reported to sell for about HK$18 million in 2016, and plates and phone numbers full of 8s regularly set records.

6: smooth (溜/顺)

六 liù sounds like 溜 "smooth" and relates to 禄 "fortune". The idiom 六六大顺 means "everything goes smoothly". Online, 666 means "awesome".

9: long-lasting (久)

九 jiǔ sounds like 久 "long time". It is the emperor's number: the Forbidden City is said to have 9,999 rooms. Couples love 9 for weddings.

2: pairs (双)

好事成双, "good things come in pairs". In Cantonese, 2 sounds like 易 "easy".

The unlucky numbers

4 (四 sì ≈ 死 sǐ, death) is the big one. Buildings skip 4, 14 and 24. Some phone makers skipped model numbers with 4. 7 is mixed: the 7th lunar month is Ghost Month. 250 means "idiot". 14 reads as "want to die", 74 as "angry to death".

Numbers in real life

  • Prices: retailers use 8s (¥88, ¥168) and avoid 4s.
  • Gifts: give items in pairs and never in fours. Red envelopes follow number rules (calculator).
  • Addresses: homes with 8 can command higher prices in Chinese-majority markets. Some cities renumber streets to avoid 4.
  • Business: firms pick 8-heavy phone numbers, launch dates and domains (numeric domains).

Regional differences

Cantonese adds meanings: 3 = 生 (life), 5 = 唔 (not), which makes 58 "won't prosper" and 38 a potential insult. In Teochew (Chaozhou) tradition 4 can sound like "happiness". Western 13 has little weight in China, though global companies often skip it too.

What number is lucky for money?

8, especially 168, 518, 888 and 8888.

What number means love in Chinese?

520 (I love you) and 1314 (for a lifetime), often combined as 5201314.
