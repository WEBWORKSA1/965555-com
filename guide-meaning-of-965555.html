What does 965555 mean in Chinese?

Decoding the number behind this site: its sounds, slang and pattern value.

Quick answer: 9 (jiǔ) sounds like 久, "long-lasting". 6 (liù) sounds like 溜, "smooth". 5555 (wǔ-wǔ-wǔ-wǔ) is internet shorthand for sobbing, 呜呜呜呜. Read together: "lasting, smooth… and a good cry". We read it as from tears to lasting fortune. The number has no 4 and no 0 and ends in a quad pattern, which makes it memorable and premium among six-digit numbers.

Folk readings

  • 久溜 + 呜呜呜呜: "lasting and smooth, then crying". Ideal for a brand that turns number worries into clarity.
  • 965 ≈ 就留我 (jiù liú wǒ): "just keep me". This is a playful homophone chain in the style of 520 = 我爱你.
  • 五福 (Five Blessings): longevity, wealth, health, virtue and a peaceful end. Four 5s after "lasting" can be read as blessings that endure.

These are informal, crowd-style readings. Number slang is creative and context-dependent, and no single reading is "official".

Pattern and market value

Six-digit .com domains ("6N") are a recognised asset class among Chinese investors. Of the one million possible 6N .coms, only 262,144 contain neither 0 nor 4 (86), and those are considered "premium". Patterns such as AAAA endings, ABAB repeats and endings in 88 sell above random numbers. 965555 combines no-0/no-4 digits with a quad ending. See our numeric domain guide.
