How Chinese families choose dates

Digits are the modern layer: couples in China flock to register marriages on dates like 5/20 (I love you) and 2013-01-04 (love you for a lifetime). Businesses love the 8th, 18th and 28th. The traditional layer is the Tong Shu (通书) almanac, which rates each lunar day for weddings, travel, moving and ground-breaking. The 7th lunar month (Ghost Month) is widely avoided for big events.
