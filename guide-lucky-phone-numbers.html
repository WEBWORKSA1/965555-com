How to choose a lucky phone number

Quick answer: Prioritise the last 4 digits. Aim for 8, 6 and 9, patterns (8888, 6688, 1688) and no 4s. Avoid 14, 74 and 748 anywhere.

The rules of thumb

  1. Endings dominate. That's what people remember.
  2. No 4 is the single biggest "value" signal.
  3. Repetition sells: AAAA > AABB > ABAB > random.
  4. Meaningful combos add personality: 520 for a florist, 168 for a trader.
  5. Read "1" as yāo, as Chinese speakers do on the phone.

Where lucky numbers come from

Carriers in China, Hong Kong, Singapore and Malaysia reserve "golden numbers" (靓号) and sell them at premiums or by auction. Secondary markets and brokers also trade them. Always use official carrier channels to transfer ownership.

Is a phone number with 4 always bad?

No. Context matters: 4 followed by 8 can be read differently, and non-Chinese contacts won't care. For Chinese-market business lines, many still avoid it.
