Red envelope etiquette

  • Never 4: no 4, 40 or 400, and no amounts ending in 4.
  • Even for joy: weddings and celebrations use even amounts (pairs).
  • Odd for grief: condolence money (帛金) uses odd amounts in white envelopes.
  • New notes: crisp bills show respect. Many people queue at banks before Lunar New Year.
  • Digital hongbao: WeChat and Alipay red packets use the same lucky numbers, such as 8.88, 52.0 and 131.4.
