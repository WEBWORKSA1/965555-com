Why numbers became slang

Early pager and SMS users in China typed on numeric keypads, and Chinese characters were slow to input. Numbers that sound like phrases became fast, playful shorthand. The habit survived into QQ, WeChat, Douyin and gaming chat. Dates now use it too: May 20 (5/20) is China's biggest online Valentine's Day, and weddings spike on 2013-01-04 (爱你一生一世).
